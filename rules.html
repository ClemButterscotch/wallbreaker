02 · Objective

The Wallfacer has a secret plan and must complete it exactly.

The plan identifies three of the six dial colors and gives an exact target value for each. The Loyal team wins when all three values match after a round resolves.

  • 01

    All three targets must match at the same time. A near miss does not count.

  • 02

    The other three dials do not affect completion. They may have any value.

  • 03

    The plan is private. Only the Wallfacer and the observer see its colors and target values during play.

04 · Setup

Every standard game uses one Wallfacer and one Wallbreaker.

At least three players are required. One player is the Wallfacer, one is the Wallbreaker, one is Shi Qiang, and every additional player is a Specialist. The host is a neutral observer and does not occupy a player seat.

  • Fixed core roles1 Wallfacer + 1 Wallbreaker + 1 Shi Qiang
  • Additional playersEvery remaining seat is a Loyal Specialist.
  • Round limitThe host chooses 6, 8, 10, or 12 rounds.

05 · Role · The Wallfacer

The Wallfacer must complete the plan without revealing its three colors.

The Wallfacer is Loyal and is the only player who sees the complete standard-game plan.

  • Private informationThe three plan colors and the exact target value for each.
  • Round actionChange any one dial by −1, 0, or +1.
  • CommunicationGuide the Loyal team without stating all three plan colors.
Why the plan cannot be announcedThe Wallbreaker can win by guessing the three colors.

The Wallbreaker does not need the values, so saying the three colors aloud would reveal everything required for a correct guess.

06 · Role · The Wallbreaker

The Wallbreaker tries to identify the three colors in the plan.

The Wallbreaker opposes the Loyal team. During each round, the Wallbreaker chooses one of two actions.

  • Affect a dialChange any one dial by −1, 0, or +1.
  • Or use the SophonDo not move a dial. After resolution, privately see which action the Wallfacer locked.
  • Final guessChoose three dial colors. A correct guess wins; an incorrect guess immediately wins for the Loyal team.

07 · Wallbreaker guess · Interactive example

The Wallbreaker guesses colors, not values.

In this example, the plan is Yellow 6, Blue 2, and Red 8. Only Yellow, Blue, and Red must be submitted in the guess.

  • 01

    A correct color set wins immediately. The three target values are irrelevant to the guess.

  • 02

    An incorrect color set ends the game. The Loyal team wins immediately.

  • 03

    This is why the Wallfacer must be careful. Publicly naming all three colors supplies the entire guess.

08 · Role · Shi Qiang

Shi Qiang either affects one dial or arrests one player.

Shi Qiang is the single Police role and is Loyal. The arrest replaces Shi Qiang’s dial action for that round.

  • Affect a dialChange any one dial by −1, 0, or +1.
  • Or arrestChoose one other player. If that player chose a dial effect, omit it from the round total.
  • Private resultOnly the arrested player is notified. The arrest and the player’s identity are not announced publicly during play.
  • ScopeAn arrest does not cancel anyone else and does not prevent a Sophon observation.

09 · Role · Specialists

Each Specialist receives one random subject specialty.

Every remaining player is a Loyal Specialist. Specialties are assigned independently from Mathematics, Science, and Agriculture.

Independent random assignmentDuplicates are allowed.

Several Specialists may receive the same subject, and a subject may receive no Specialist.

  • Inside the specialtyUse −2, −1, 0, +1, or +2 on either of that subject’s two dials.
  • Outside the specialtyUse only −1, 0, or +1.

10 · Round sequence

A round has four steps.

All legal dial effects are added together before anything is revealed. The public board shows only each dial’s net change.

01

Discuss

Players may discuss the public board and possible actions.

→
02

Choose

Each player privately selects one legal dial or special action.

→
03

Lock

A locked player may switch which action is locked, but cannot return to being unlocked.

→
04

Resolve

Once everyone is locked, the game submits and resolves whichever action each player currently has locked.

Effects add+2 + +1 = +3

The dial’s public change is +3.

Effects cancel+1 + −1 = 0

This is indistinguishable from nobody affecting that dial.

Dials stop at 0 and 98 + 3 → 9

The value is clamped at 9, not increased to 11.

12 · Victory conditions

The plan, the guess, or the clock.

Loyal winThe plan is completed

After resolution, all three plan dials exactly match their target values.

Wallbreaker winThe three colors are guessed

The Wallbreaker correctly identifies all three plan colors. Values are not required.

Loyal winThe guess is wrong

Any incorrect set of three colors ends the game immediately.

Wallbreaker winThe round limit is reached

The final configured round ends without the plan being completed.

13 · Observer

The observer runs the room but does not play.

The observer browser holds the authoritative game state. It validates locked actions, resolves rounds, checks victory, and broadcasts the public result.

  • 01

    The observer sees roles and the plan. This information stays hidden from players during a standard game.

  • 02

    Messaging is optional and private. Each player may message the observer, not the entire table.

  • 03

    Disconnected players can be resolved as no move. This prevents a round from remaining locked indefinitely.

  • 04

    Postgame disclosure is public. Roles, the plan, the final guess, arrests, and round history are revealed during the recap.

14 · Guided practice

Guided practice uses the same rules with hidden information exposed.

Use guided practice to teach the game. The role composition, legal actions, resolution, and victory conditions remain the same.

  • 01

    Roles and the plan are visible. Players can connect each role’s information to its decisions.

  • 02

    Locked actions are visible. The group can inspect how the public net change was produced.

  • 03

    Standard games remain private. The extra teaching information is never sent in a normal game.

Before starting, confirm these rules.

  3. 3

  4. 4
