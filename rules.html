Wallbreaker
rulebook

Wallbreaker is a social deduction game. The Wallfacer team changes six public dials to complete a secret plan while the Wallbreaker team tries to identify which three colors matter.

01 · Teams and goals

There are two teams.

Wallfacer team

Led by the Wallfacer. Complete the plan.

Wallbreaker team

The Wallbreaker is all alone. Guess the plan’s three colors.

02 · The board

The board has six colored dials.

Every dial displays a whole number from 0 through 9. Everyone sees the same current values.

  • Dial limitsA dial cannot move below 0 or above 9.
03 · Round structure

04 · The Wallfacer · The plan

Only the Wallfacer sees the complete plan.

Their screen outlines the three plan dials and labels each exact target.

  • Complete the planAll three marked dials must match their targets after the same round.
  • Ignore the other dialsThe other three values do not affect completion.
05 · The Wallfacer · Playing

Guide the Wallfacer team without naming all three colors.

Each round, move one dial by −1 or +1. Passing is not allowed.

Why the plan stays secretThe Wallbreaker only needs the three colors.

Saying all three colors aloud gives the Wallbreaker the complete guess.

06 · The Wallbreaker

The Wallbreaker is alone.

Each round, choose one of two actions.

  • Affect a dialMove one dial by −1 or +1.
  • Or use Sophon observationMove no dial. After resolution, privately see the action the Wallfacer locked.
  • Guess the planGuess three colors at any time.
07 · Wallbreaker guess

A correct guess wins. A wrong guess loses.

Every guess ends the game immediately.

Guess the plan

If this guess is right, you win. If it is wrong, you lose.

08 · Shi Qiang · Making an arrest

Shi Qiang may choose not to move a dial.

Instead, Shi Qiang can arrest one other player and cancel that player’s dial move.

  • Cancelled this roundIf they locked a dial effect, it is left out of the round total.
  • Known only to themOnly the arrested player is notified. Sophon observation still works.
09 · Shi Qiang · Private result

The arrested player gets a private popup.

Their locked dial effect is cancelled for the round. Everyone else sees only the combined result.

  • Dismiss and continueThe player closes the popup and keeps playing normally.
  • Revealed after the gameThe arrest and the player’s identity appear in the postgame recap.
10 · Role · Specialists

Each Specialist receives one random subject specialty.

Every remaining player is a Specialist on the Wallfacer team. Specialties are assigned independently from Mathematics, Science, and Agriculture.

Duplicates are allowed.
  • Inside the specialtyUse −2, −1, +1, or +2 on either of that subject’s two dials.
  • Outside the specialtyUse only −1 or +1. Passing is not allowed.
11 · Victory conditions

The game ends when:

Wallfacer teamThe plan is completed

After resolution, all three plan dials exactly match their target values.

Wallbreaker teamThe three colors are guessed

The Wallbreaker correctly identifies all three plan colors. Values are not required.

Wallfacer teamThe guess is wrong

Any incorrect set of three colors ends the game immediately.

Wallbreaker teamThe round limit is reached

The final round ends without the plan being completed.

12 · Advanced play variant

Wild Roles replace Specialists.

There are no Specialists in this variant. Instead, those players receive different Loyal Wild Roles. Each begins with a private task—a bit of an ulterior motive at first—but still needs the Wallfacer team to win.

01Private taskSatisfy your Wild goal

Your goal may depend on arrests, dial positions, or your own moves.

→
02Shared workHelp the Wallfacer team

Wild players never see the plan or its target values.

→
03Shared finishWin together

A Wild Role wins only if their private task is complete and the Wallfacer team wins.

13 · Wild Roles · Information

The role bank is public. Assignments stay secret.

Anyone may use the Wild roles button during play to reread the nine generic goals and their completion rules.

  • PublicThe nine possible roles and their generic goals.
  • PrivateYour identity, assigned targets or dials, and progress toward your Wild goal.
  • Wallbreaker cover roleAt least one role is always unoccupied. The Wallbreaker learns exactly one of them and may pretend to be it.
  • The plan stays privateWild players never receive its target values or a plan display. Some roles identify off-plan dials. Shi Qiang receives no special Wild Role information.
