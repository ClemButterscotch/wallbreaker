01 · Objective

Raise three secret fields to 7—and keep them there.

The Wallfacer secretly receives three named fields. The Loyal team wins only when all three remain at or above 7 after the Wallbreaker’s decay resolves.

  • 01

    The plan names fields, not exact values. A plan might be Panopticon, Lemon, and Game Theory.

  • 02

    Every value at 7 or higher counts. The three fields do not need to match each other.

  • 03

    Decay gets the final word. Touching 7 during advancement is not enough if decay pulls a plan field back down.

03 · Roles

One Wallfacer. One Wallbreaker. Everyone else specializes.

Mathbreaker needs at least three players. There is no Police role, no Civilians, and no round limit.

WF
Wallfacer

Advance any one field by +1.

Knows the three-field plan and guides the table without revealing it.

SP
Specialist

Specialty +2, or any other field +1.

Knows only a private specialty and tries to infer which fields matter.

WB
Wallbreaker

Assign one −1 decay per good player.

May stack multiple decay on one field, then privately test a three-field combination.

Decay budgetTotal players − 1=everyone except the Wallbreaker

06 · Wallbreaker guesses

Test one unused combination per turn.

  • 01

    Choose exactly three different fields. Values do not matter; only the field combination matters.

  • 02

    Wrong guesses stay private. The table is not told, but that combination can never be submitted again.

  • 03

    One guess each turn. Guessing remains available after decay is locked, but closes when resolution begins.

  • 04

    Decay and guessing are independent. One never consumes or replaces the other.

07 · Victory

Hold three fields at 7—or name all three.

  • 01

    Loyal victory. After decay, all three secret plan fields are at 7 or higher.

  • 02

    Wallbreaker victory. A correct private combination alerts the Wallbreaker and Wallfacer.

  • 03

    The declaration finishes it. The Wallbreaker names the Wallfacer and reveals all three fields aloud before confirming.

  • 04

    No clock runs out. Mathbreaker has no round-limit victory condition.
